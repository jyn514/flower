i’m feeling cynical about computers again.

what is the purpose of a computer? “the purpose of a system is what it does”, Stafford Beer tells us. what does a computer do?

at a first order: computers automate labor. computers [shrink time and distance][no neutral ground]. computers execute orders, [without remorse](https://idiallo.com/blog/when-a-machine-fired-me)
or accountability. <!-- todo ibm management decision -->

at a second order: [computers automate workers out of a job][alex]. computers create new social graphs. computers allow organizations to deny responsibility. computers increase stability.

[alex]: https://bsky.app/profile/jyn.dev/post/3lcbspwtoss24

at a third order: [computers automate away industries][hidden-figures]. computers weird politics. computers [push][siderea insurance] [coordinative communication] [onto individuals][computational tyranny]. computers [decrease metastability][mcas].

i’ll talk about what all that means in a second. but first i want to say: if you are a programmer building tools used at scale, you are creating the levers of [control][what is cybernetics]. if you make 6 figures, [you have power][billionaire math]. 

[what is cybernetics]: https://tech.lgbt/@jyn/111965154970521565

who do we look up to? who shows us healthy, kind, just examples of exercising power? <!-- Superman and Luke Skywalker are common in popular culture.
